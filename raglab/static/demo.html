How it works

  • Chunking: each article is split into paragraphs.
  • Hybrid retrieval: BM25 (keyword match) and a bge-small embedding model each rank the paragraphs; reciprocal rank fusion combines the two lists.
  • Reranking: a MiniLM cross-encoder rescores the top 20 by reading the question and passage together.
  • Reader: the top 3 passages go to Qwen3-0.6B, told to answer with the shortest exact phrase or say it cannot. The extractive option skips the LLM and returns the best-matching sentence.
  • Guardrails: prompt-injection checks on the question and passages, and a grounding check that blocks answers not supported by the passages.

What the evaluation found

  • For questions that have an answer, this retrieval pipeline ranks a passage containing it first 90% of the time (Recall@1 0.900, nDCG@10 0.949).
  • The reader is the weak link: Qwen3-0.6B often gives a partial answer or abstains even when the right passage is there. SQuAD 2.0 also has "trick" questions with no answer in the article (one is always among the examples); there the right behaviour is to abstain.
  • Running on a free 2-CPU server, so the LLM answer can take 10–20 seconds. The passages appear first.
